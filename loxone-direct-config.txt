LOXONE DIRECT CONTROL OF THE 10 SAMSUNG TVs - no bridge, Miniserver only
Generated 2026-07-27 after live testing against the TVs.

WHAT WORKS DIRECT:  power ON (wol://), volume + mute (tcp:// UPnP),
                    app launch (http:// port 8001).
NOT POSSIBLE DIRECT: power OFF, HDMI source switching, remote keys.
  These 2025 Tizen TVs only accept those over a token-paired TLS
  WebSocket (wss :8002). Verified: legacy port 55000 closed, plain
  ws :8001 answers ms.channel.unauthorized. A Miniserver has no
  WebSocket client, so no template or command string can ever do it.

==========================================================================
1) POWER ON - Wake on LAN (native Loxone)
==========================================================================
One Virtual Output, Address:  wol://
Then one Virtual Output Command per TV; 'Command when ON' = MAC:

  Golf Sim Right  00:7d:3b:3c:5b:bb     (IP 192.168.1.88)
  Golf Sim Left   b8:a0:b8:49:d7:fe     (IP 192.168.1.107)
  Bar Side        b8:a0:b8:49:d6:36     (IP 192.168.1.58)

  Kitchen Frame   74:6d:fa:38:75:2e     (IP 192.168.1.127)

TV setting required once per TV: Settings > Connection > Network >
Expert Settings > Power On with Mobile = ON (a.k.a. network standby).
Pavillion + Kitchen Frame are on WiFi - WoL from cold power is not
reliable over WiFi; all other TVs are wired and fine.
